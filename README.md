# CampusKit

A simple student-tools website designed around three monetization paths:
1. Display advertising
2. Affiliate marketing
3. Original digital products

## Launch
Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` to any static hosting service.

## Before monetizing
- Replace placeholder affiliate links with your own approved links.
- Add your own privacy policy, terms, and affiliate disclosure.
- Apply to an ad network when your site meets its current eligibility requirements.
- Create original content around the tools so the site provides genuine value.
- Do not click your own ads or use artificial traffic.

## Suggested growth
Publish SEO pages for specific student searches such as:
- CGPA calculator
- semester GPA calculator
- percentage calculator
- Pomodoro timer
- study planner
- placement preparation checklist

This does not guarantee income; traffic, conversion rates, platform approval, and audience demand determine revenue.
