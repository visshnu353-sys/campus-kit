The idea behind CampusKit

CampusKit was created around a simple idea: student tools should be easy to discover, quick to use and useful enough to return to every week.

Instead of sending students across unrelated websites for basic calculations, planning and preparation, CampusKit aims to provide a single student-focused workspace that grows with their academic journey.

  • Simple interfaces designed around common student workflows
  • Practical tools that work directly in the browser
  • Useful resources for academics, productivity and careers
  • A continuously expanding collection of student utilities
