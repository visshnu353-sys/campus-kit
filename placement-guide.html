CAREER / ROADMAP

Placement Preparation Guide

A practical roadmap for students preparing for software engineering and campus placement opportunities.

Build fundamentals

Study data structures and algorithms, object-oriented programming, databases, operating systems and computer networks.

Build projects

Create projects that solve real problems and document your technical decisions clearly.

Prepare your resume

Use concise project descriptions, relevant skills and professional profile links.

Practice interviews

Practice coding, technical explanations and behavioral questions, then review mistakes.
